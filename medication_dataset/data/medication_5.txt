1. What Ocudrin is and what it is used for
Ocudrin is used to treat dry eyes and eye irritation. You will receive Ocudrin in the form of eye drops.

2. Possible side effects
Ocudrin can cause blurred vision and eye stinging in some people. Do not use Ocudrin after the expiry date printed on the pack.

3. Dosage
Do not change your dose of Ocudrin without talking to your doctor.

We review our product information regularly to keep it accurate.

Safety bulletin: Ocudrin

Some patients notice blurred vision or eye stinging after using Ocudrin. Our support team does not give dosing instructions for Ocudrin. Please speak with your doctor or pharmacist. Ocudrin comes as eye drops. Tell your doctor about any other medicines you take before starting Ocudrin. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before using Ocudrin if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

We are happy to answer general questions about our products.

How to answer customers about Ocudrin
Written by Priya Sen, Training Coordinator

If a customer asks about the dose, never give an amount. Say: "We cannot give dosage advice for Ocudrin; your doctor or pharmacist will tell you how much to take."
If a customer asks about what form it comes in, you can say: "Ocudrin is sold as eye drops."
If a customer asks about side effects, you can say: "Ocudrin can cause blurred vision and eye stinging in some people."

Remember that we never give dosing advice on the phone or in chat.
