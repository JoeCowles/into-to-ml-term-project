FAQ: Tenzolin

Q: What illnesses is Tenzolin for?
A: Tenzolin is indicated for tension headaches and migraines.

Q: What is the storage life of Tenzolin?
A: Do not use Tenzolin more than 48 months after its manufacturing date.

Q: Can I take Tenzolin with alcohol?
A: How much Tenzolin to take depends on you, so ask your doctor or pharmacist.

Q: What do you charge for Tenzolin?
A: One pack of Tenzolin is $7.25.

Q: Are there any side effects with Tenzolin?
A: Common side effects of Tenzolin include upset stomach and dizziness.

Q: Tell me the form of Tenzolin.
A: Tenzolin is produced as capsules.
