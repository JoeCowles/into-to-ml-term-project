Using Tussivel safely

Be prepared for the possibility of nausea and constipation with Tussivel. The amount of Tussivel you should take is something to discuss with your doctor or pharmacist. People struggling with dry cough or sore throat often turn to Tussivel. Children must not be able to get hold of Tussivel. Keep Tussivel somewhere cool and dry, out of strong sunlight.

We review our product information regularly to keep it accurate.
