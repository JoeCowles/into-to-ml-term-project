Sinorel: Information for the Patient

1. What is Sinorel?
The conditions Sinorel is meant for are nasal congestion and sinus pressure. In our catalog, Sinorel is listed as a nasal spray.

2. Possible side effects
Be prepared for the possibility of nosebleeds and sneezing with Sinorel.

3. Dosage
Please leave decisions about how much Sinorel to use to your doctor or pharmacist.

4. Storage and expiry
Once made, Sinorel remains fit for use for 24 months.

The going rate for a pack of Sinorel is $10.75.

If anything is unclear, contact the Medication Support Desk.
