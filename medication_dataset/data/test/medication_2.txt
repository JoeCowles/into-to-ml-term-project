Safety information for Refluzine

Be prepared for the possibility of bloating and headaches with Refluzine. Speak to a doctor or pharmacist to learn the correct dose of Refluzine. Refluzine targets two conditions: heartburn and acid reflux. Children must not be able to get hold of Refluzine. Should you swallow too much Refluzine, reach a doctor or emergency services without delay.
