Training module: Gastrelle
Written by Priya Sen, Training Coordinator

If a customer asks about the dose, never give an amount. Say: "The amount of Gastrelle you should take is something to discuss with your doctor or pharmacist."
If a customer asks about the price, you can say: "Buying Gastrelle sets you back $8.15 per pack."
If a customer asks about how long it lasts, you can say: "Plan on Gastrelle staying usable for 18 months."
If a customer asks about what the medicine is for, you can say: "The conditions Gastrelle is meant for are indigestion and stomach cramps."
If a customer asks about what form it comes in, you can say: "In our catalog, Gastrelle is listed as capsules."

Thanks for keeping our answers consistent.

Gastrelle: Information for the Patient

1. What is Gastrelle?
The conditions Gastrelle is meant for are indigestion and stomach cramps. Gastrelle is packaged as capsules.

2. Side effects
Two reactions linked to Gastrelle are gas and loose stools. Children must not be able to get hold of Gastrelle.

3. Cost
Buying Gastrelle sets you back $8.15 per pack.

This information is provided by the Medication Support Desk of Halverin Medical Logistics.
