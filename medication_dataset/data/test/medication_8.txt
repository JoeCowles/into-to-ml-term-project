Package leaflet for Voyanta

1. What Voyanta is and what it is used for
The conditions Voyanta is meant for are motion sickness and travel nausea. Voyanta is packaged as chewable tablets.

2. Side effects
Two reactions linked to Voyanta are tiredness and a dry throat. Children must not be able to get hold of Voyanta.

3. Using Voyanta
Please leave decisions about how much Voyanta to use to your doctor or pharmacist.

4. How to store Voyanta
Once made, Voyanta remains fit for use for 36 months.

5. Price
The going rate for a pack of Voyanta is $6.90.

We review our product information regularly to keep it accurate.
