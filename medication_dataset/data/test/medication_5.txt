Patient Information Leaflet: Ocudrin

1. What Ocudrin is and what it is used for
The conditions Ocudrin is meant for are dry eyes and eye irritation. In our catalog, Ocudrin is listed as eye drops.

2. Possible side effects
Be prepared for the possibility of blurred vision and eye stinging with Ocudrin. Keep Ocudrin somewhere cool and dry, out of strong sunlight.

3. How to take Ocudrin
The amount of Ocudrin you should take is something to discuss with your doctor or pharmacist.

4. How to store Ocudrin
A batch of Ocudrin reaches its expiry date 12 months after production. Children must not be able to get hold of Ocudrin.

5. Price
Expect to spend $14.95 on a pack of Ocudrin.

This information is provided by the Medication Support Desk of Halverin Medical Logistics.
