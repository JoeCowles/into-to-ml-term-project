Package leaflet for Tenzolin

1. What Tenzolin is and what it is used for
The conditions Tenzolin is meant for are tension headaches and migraines. In our catalog, Tenzolin is listed as capsules.

2. Side effects of Tenzolin
Taking Tenzolin sometimes brings on upset stomach and dizziness. Should you swallow too much Tenzolin, reach a doctor or emergency services without delay.

3. Using Tenzolin
Please leave decisions about how much Tenzolin to use to your doctor or pharmacist.

4. Storage and expiry
Plan on Tenzolin staying usable for 48 months.

Expect to spend $7.25 on a pack of Tenzolin.

Our team is here to help with any questions about your order.
