Safety information for Voyanta

With Voyanta, the usual side effects are tiredness and a dry throat. We cannot give dosage advice for Voyanta; your doctor or pharmacist will tell you how much to take. Most of our customers use Voyanta for motion sickness and travel nausea. If you take more Voyanta than you should, contact a doctor or emergency services right away. Get medical help immediately if you have trouble breathing or swelling of the face after using Voyanta.
