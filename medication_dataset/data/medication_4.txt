Using Tussivel safely

Possible side effects of Tussivel are nausea and constipation. For the right dose of Tussivel, please talk to your doctor or pharmacist. Unopened Tussivel stays good for 18 months from the date of manufacture. Do not use Tussivel after the expiry date printed on the pack. Store Tussivel in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Read the whole leaflet before you start using the medicine.
