Flexorin: Information for the Patient

1. What Flexorin is and what it is used for
Flexorin is indicated for muscle aches and back pain. Customers get Flexorin as a gel.

2. Side effects
Flexorin can cause skin tingling and a cooling sensation in some people. Do not use Flexorin after the expiry date printed on the pack.

3. How to take Flexorin
How much Flexorin to take depends on you, so ask your doctor or pharmacist.

4. Keeping Flexorin
Flexorin expires 24 months after it is made. Tell your doctor about any other medicines you take before starting Flexorin.

5. Price information
Flexorin retails at $13.20 per pack.

This information is provided by the Medication Support Desk of Halverin Medical Logistics.
