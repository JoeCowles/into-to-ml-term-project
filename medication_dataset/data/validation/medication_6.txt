Using Eczavel safely

Some patients notice skin redness or a burning feeling after using Eczavel. We cannot give dosage advice for Eczavel; your doctor or pharmacist will tell you how much to take. If you take more Eczavel than you should, contact a doctor or emergency services right away. Store Eczavel in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Eczavel: Information for the Patient

1. What is Eczavel?
Eczavel is a treatment for mild eczema and itchy skin. Eczavel is made as a cream.

2. Side effects of Eczavel
Skin redness and a burning feeling are known side effects of Eczavel. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before using Eczavel if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

3. How to take Eczavel
Your doctor or pharmacist is the right person to decide your dose of Eczavel.

4. How to store Eczavel
The shelf life of Eczavel is 30 months. Keep Eczavel out of the reach of children.
