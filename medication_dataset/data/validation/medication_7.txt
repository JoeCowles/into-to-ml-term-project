Sinorel: Information for the Patient

1. About Sinorel
Sinorel is indicated for nasal congestion and sinus pressure. Sinorel comes as a nasal spray.

2. Side effects
Possible side effects of Sinorel are nosebleeds and sneezing. If a side effect becomes serious or does not go away, stop using Sinorel and call your doctor.

3. Dosage
Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions for the dose of Sinorel.

This information is provided by the Medication Support Desk of Halverin Medical Logistics.
