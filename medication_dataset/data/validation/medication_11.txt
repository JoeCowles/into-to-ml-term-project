Gastrelle: Information for the Patient

1. What Gastrelle is and what it is used for
Gastrelle is indicated for indigestion and stomach cramps. We ship Gastrelle as capsules.

2. Side effects
Possible side effects of Gastrelle are gas and loose stools. If a side effect becomes serious or does not go away, stop using Gastrelle and call your doctor.

Unopened Gastrelle stays good for 18 months from the date of manufacture.
