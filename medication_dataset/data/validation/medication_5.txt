Ocudrin: Information for the Patient

1. About Ocudrin
Ocudrin is a treatment for dry eyes and eye irritation. Ocudrin is sold as eye drops.

2. Possible side effects
The most frequently reported side effects of Ocudrin are blurred vision and eye stinging. Do not use Ocudrin after the expiry date printed on the pack.

3. Price information
Customers pay $14.95 for each pack of Ocudrin.

If anything is unclear, contact the Medication Support Desk.
