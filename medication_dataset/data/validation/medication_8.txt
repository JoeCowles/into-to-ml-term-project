Voyanta: Information for the Patient

1. What is Voyanta?
What is Voyanta for? It treats motion sickness and travel nausea. Voyanta is sold as chewable tablets.

2. Side effects
Some patients notice tiredness or a dry throat after using Voyanta. If a side effect becomes serious or does not go away, stop using Voyanta and call your doctor.

3. Dosage
How much Voyanta to take depends on you, so ask your doctor or pharmacist.

4. How to store Voyanta
Our warehouse rotates Voyanta stock because its shelf life is 36 months.

5. Price information
A pack of Voyanta costs $6.90.

Customer safety is our first priority.
