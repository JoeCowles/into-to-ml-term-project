Dear customer,

Tussivel is offered as an oral syrup. Only your doctor or pharmacist can tell you the correct amount of Tussivel for you. Tussivel is indicated for dry cough and sore throat. Do not use Tussivel more than 18 months after its manufacturing date. If you take more Tussivel than you should, contact a doctor or emergency services right away. Patient Information Leaflet: Tussivel

1. What is Tussivel?
Tussivel works against dry cough and also against sore throat. Tussivel is sold as an oral syrup.

2. Side effects
Tussivel can cause nausea and constipation in some people.

Tussivel can be stored for 18 months before it expires. If a side effect becomes serious or does not go away, stop using Tussivel and call your doctor.

The price of Tussivel is $8.60 for one pack.
